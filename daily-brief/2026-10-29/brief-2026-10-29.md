## 1. Consultative Selling for Technical B2B — The Hanan PIP Framework

**Source:** Mack Hanan, *Consultative Selling* (8th ed., AMACOM) — the original framework (1970) used by IBM, HP, Boeing, Motorola, GM.

### Key Insights
- **Core shift:** Customers don't buy products; they buy improved business outcomes. A vendor sells at product price; a consultant sells at outcome value.
- **Profit Improvement Proposal (PIP):** One-page document showing the customer's exact financial return — investment required, revenue gained/costs reduced, payback period. Replaces traditional spec+price proposals.
- **Three strategies:** (1) Position as value-adder, not vendor. (2) Propose financial value in quantified, time-framed terms. (3) Partner with customer operating manager to co-manage realization.
- **Two-tiered sales model:** Separate consultative sales (high-margin, outcome-based) from commodity sales (price-driven). S5's safety-grade CM + assurance is pure consultative tier.
- **Hit ratio:** PIPping achieves ~1:1 because the customer becomes pre-invested in the joint business case.

### Concrete Application to S5

|------------------|-----------------|
| RotaMon/WearMon sensors + CFSE/NER assurance | "Extend proof-test interval 12→24 months on conveyor SIFs → halves PFDavg → saves $X/yr in shutdown labour + avoids $Y/yr in lost production" |
| Audit-traceable condition records for MSMS compliance | "Reduces WorkSafe prosecution exposure from 'worsening pattern' fines ($810k FQM precedent) to documented evidence defence" |
| Criticality ranking + CMMS integration | "Frees 0.5 FTE reliability engineer per site → redeployed to higher-value RCM/FMECA work" |

## 2. Customer Validation for Hardware + Services — 5-Step Deep-Tech Framework

**Source:** Validate & Launch, *The 5-Step Validation Framework for Hardware & Deep-Tech Startups* (Simon, founder of 4 shipped products); MIT Sloan / IESE / Harvard Business School validation research.

### Key Insights
- **Hardware intensity changes validation, not whether you validate:** Iteration costs compound — every wrong assumption burns runway.
- **Step 1 (Problem):** 20 discovery interviews, bottom-up market sizing (nameable customers × willingness-to-pay), search intent cross-check. Validated when 8/10 describe same pain unprompted.
- **Step 2 (Solution):** Minimum viable prototype at TRL 3-4 (lab demo), not TRL 5-6. Use digital twins, Wizard of Oz, service simulations. Answer: "What result would prove this does NOT work?"
- **Step 3 (Market):** Landing page + waitlist (15%+ cold conversion = signal). Collect 5-10 LOIs from credible buyers. Map regulatory path (IEC 61511, WA MSMS) NOW, not after build.
- **Step 4 (Unit Economics):** Prototype COGS often 10× production. Validate credible path to target COGS + CAC/LTV at scale. 90-day founder-led sales: 50 outreach → 20 demos → 2 paid pilots.
- **Step 5 (Scale):** Formal metrics dashboard from day one (interviews, LOI conversion, pilot close rate, COGS vs target, TRL, regulatory milestones). Secure design partners (co-develop, test access, case studies).

### Key Insights
1. **Long-term focus:** Address full contract life — transition in/out plans, continuous improvement, foresee Year 2-3 challenges.
2. **Detail experience as case studies:** Challenges faced + how overcome + team names/roles + similarities to proposed work. Cover safety, QA, mobilisation, continuous improvement.

5. **Value time AND money:** Quantify opportunity cost of downtime ($2k part → $100k/hr lost production). Speed > price for critical spares.

## 4. Functional Safety Consulting Business Model — The IEC 61511 Services Lifecycle

**Source:** Yokogawa Functional Safety Consultancy Services (global leader, TÜV Rheinland certified); IEC 61511-1:2026 (published Jul 2026).

### Key Insights
- **Service portfolio spans entire safety lifecycle:** HAZOP → SIL Classification → SIL Verification → SRS → SIS Validation → Proof Testing → FS Management System → FS Assessments → FS Audits → Training.
- **Plant owner legally responsible** for entire lifecycle (planning to disposal). They *must* buy assurance — it's not optional.
- **IEC 61511:2026 new requirements** (published Jul 2026): systematic capability demonstration, formal FS management systems, competence management, **security risk assessments** (malicious interference), independent assessment of modifications, better failure rate substantiation.
- **Proof testing is "obligatory and often onerous"** — operators struggle with it. Extending proof-test interval (12→24 months) via diagnostic coverage evidence = direct OPEX savings.
- **No sensor vendor does this.** No safety consultancy ships hardware. S5's wedge is exactly this gap.
