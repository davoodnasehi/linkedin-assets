# S5 System — LinkedIn Sample Post (Case A: Fixed Plant SCADA Upgrade)

## Final Post Copy (publish text)

A single unplanned crusher shutdown in the Pilbara can cost more than a quarter's maintenance budget.

A tier-1 iron ore producer was living with that risk every day: a legacy SCADA system on a 45 Mtpa fixed plant that operators could not fully trust, process upsets that kept recurring, and spare parts that were getting harder to source.

S5 System migrated more than 8,000 I/O points to a modern Ignition SCADA platform, rebuilt the HMI around ISA-101 standards, and tied the plant into SAP maintenance management. For the first time, the fixed-plant team had one source of truth they could act on in real time.

The result: 15% fewer process upsets, obsolescence risk removed, and operators who could see what was happening before it became an emergency.

If your fixed plant is running on ageing SCADA or fragmented data, let's talk. Message S5 System here on LinkedIn, or email us at support@s5consultant.com.au and mention "SCADA" so we can connect you with the right engineer.
